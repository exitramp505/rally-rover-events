Who we are

What the RSVP form collects

Your email address, an optional first name, which event you are interested in, and your consent.

How we use it

Only to email you updates about the event you chose and about our merchandise, as you agreed when you ticked the consent box ("Send me updates about this event and merch. Unsubscribe anytime."). We do not sell or share your email.

Where it is processed

Form submissions are relayed by the form service FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) to our project inbox. This site has no analytics or tracking cookies. Links to the Printful stores and official event sites are governed by those sites' own policies.

Unsubscribe or delete

Every email will tell you how to unsubscribe. You can also email rallyrover@mail.grokbot.com at any time to ask us to remove or delete your information, and we will do so.
