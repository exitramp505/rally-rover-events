Flower Mound glows. On Saturday, December 5, 2026, the Town's Christmas Parade and Tree Lighting Ceremony fills Gerault Park (1200 Gerault Rd.) with festivities starting at 5:30 PM. The parade rolls at 6:00 PM from Spinks Road and Garden Ridge and finishes at Gerault Park near the Community Activity Center. Then the tree lights up, local groups perform, Santa takes pictures, and there are food trucks, kids' activities, a community tent, and a fireworks show. This year's theme is A Star-Spangled Christmas, celebrating 250 years of freedom and festivity. It's free, and it's made for families.
