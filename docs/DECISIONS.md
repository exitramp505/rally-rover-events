# Decisions and history (as of 2026-10-18)

1. **Concept**: independent fan guide + merch for community parades. Merch via Printful Quick Stores (one per event). Owner: Lysander; coordination bot ("Grok Bot" lead coordinator) runs events, with Event Scriptwriter and Event Designer bots.
2. **Eventbrite attempt failed.** We published three "Fan Guide & Free RSVP" listings. Eventbrite then emailed (about 9:55 AM ET, Oct 18) that listings violated its Spam Policy, locked the account for repeated violations, unpublished the events and refunded any ticket holders. Likely cause (inference): third-party fan guides for other organizers' parades with merch links. The appeal link was EU-framed. Decision: do NOT open a second Eventbrite account to evade the lock.
3. **Replacement channels** (approved by Lysander): Luma, Facebook events, and our own website. See `channels_research.md`.
4. **Independent-promoter framing**: never imply we are the organizer or sell tickets. Title does not copy the official name as a ticketed event; official event link comes first; merch is a separate "Take it home" section; disclaimer is the last line of every piece ("Independent fan guide and merchandise; not affiliated with or endorsed by the event organizers."). RSVP is interest-only, not a ticket.
5. **Vibe and city notes** per event (see `vibe_city_notes.md`): every brief to Scriptwriter/Designer carries a vibe note (one line, 3-5 mood words, colors) and, for city-tied events, a local-connection note (real landmarks, colors, traditions; no logos or trademarks of others).
6. **Price plan** (approved by Lysander 2026-10-18, applied to all three stores): size-based pricing +$2 per size step from 2XL on tees and hoodies; tote $25; poster $26; mug $18 and ornament $16 unchanged; bundles tee+mug $42 and ornament+mug $30 approved but unavailable natively. Margins before the change (Printful profit, shipping paid by customer): tee $15 (5XL $7), hoodie $25 to $17, mug $12, poster $11, ornament $10, tote $6. Stripe fees not visible in Printful.
7. **QC checklist** (owner: coordinator; nothing publishes without passing): (1) facts match master record; (2) prices match current list; (3) vibe/city match each other and the notes; (4) very short disclaimer at the very end and clear call to buy merch; (5) quality: nothing generic, no typos, readable, original art only. Two revision rounds max, then coordinator decides. Corrections of wrong live info go out immediately and are checked afterwards (Lysander approved "fix first, tell me after").
8. **Site hosting**: static site on GitHub Pages (free, relative links so it can move). Repo is under the `exitramp505` GitHub account (also Lysander's Ramble Rider account), so it shows under that name.
9. **Form service**: Web3Forms was first choice (250/mo free, CSV export, dashboard) but signup is gated by a Cloudflare "verify you are human" check that a bot must not bypass. Chose **FormSubmit**: free, no account, activation by email click, supports honeypot and AJAX. Tradeoff: no dashboard; signups arrive as emails in the project inbox (export = compile from those emails). If Lysander wants a dashboard/CSV, he can sign up for Web3Forms himself and swap the endpoint in `build.py`.
10. **No paid plans, no personal accounts** used for the site/form; the Rally Rover inbox was used for verification.
