Richmond Christmas Parade

10th & Marshall Streets to Broad Street, Richmond, VA · Free to attend

About the day

RVA, meet your Saturday morning. The 43rd annual Richmond Christmas Parade steps off Saturday, December 5, 2026 at 10:15 AM at 10th and Marshall Streets, turns onto Broad Street, and marches west through historic downtown to Allen Avenue. Rain or shine, expect colorful floats, giant helium balloons, high school and college marching bands, and Santa himself. New this year: a free fan zone with activities and food vendors near 8th and Broad Streets. Any spot along Broad Street makes a great place to watch.
